MOBILE:

- Single-column hero or carefully stacked layout.
- Hero image should remain large and beautiful.
- Navigation becomes a hamburger menu.
- Buttons should be easy to tap.
- No text overflow.
- No horizontal scrolling.
- Product grid should remain easy to browse.
- Images must not become distorted.

TABLET:

- Use 2-column layouts where appropriate.
- Do not simply shrink the desktop version.
- Maintain comfortable spacing.

DESKTOP:

- Use generous whitespace.
- Use asymmetric editorial compositions where appropriate.
- Keep product browsing efficient.

==================================================
INTERACTIONS
==================================================

Use subtle professional animations only.

Examples:

- Product image zoom on hover.
- Buttons have smooth color transitions.
- Category arrows move slightly.
- Cards lift very subtly.
- Navigation transitions smoothly.

Avoid:

- excessive bouncing
- floating objects everywhere
- heavy parallax
- distracting animations
